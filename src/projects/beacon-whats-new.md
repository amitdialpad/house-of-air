## The problem

People rarely stop their work to read a separate changelog. They usually discover that something changed only when an old pattern no longer behaves as expected.

## What I built

A "What's New" drawer inside the tool itself. It brings recent releases to the place where people already work, with short explanations instead of a wall of technical notes.

## What makes it useful

The feed updates from published release metadata, so it does not depend on someone remembering to copy the same announcement into another place.
