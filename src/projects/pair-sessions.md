## Why I built it

People kept asking how I work with AI tools. Sending links and videos did not stick because the change is bigger than learning a particular tool. It is a different working rhythm, and the easiest way to understand it is to try it with someone.

## What it is

Three structured sessions, like pair programming for designers. Session 1 is "my turn". The facilitator walks through a real, in-flight task while you watch and interrupt anytime. Session 2 flips: you drive. Session 3 is on shipping, design judgment, and accessibility.

The sessions cover the full rhythm: understanding the problem, gathering context, shaping requirements, sketching the flow, building in small slices, and preparing the work to share. Not slides. Not a prepared demo. Real work.

## How it lives

A small self-guided site. Anyone can read the sessions on their own, but the value is doing them with a partner. No certificates, no homework. Just a structured way to pick up the new rhythm by practising it.

## Day to day

Run a session when a designer asks. Update the content when the toolkit changes.
